# Synthetic event and evidence contracts

`dragonhydra.live-event/1` requires exactly `schema_version`, `event_id`, `source_id`, `match_id`, `event_type`, `event_time`, `received_at`, `sequence`, `revision`, `participant`, `attributes`, `source_mode` and `quality_state`. All identities must begin `virtual-`; modes are `DEMONSTRATION` or `REPLAY`. The word `live` in the versioned schema is an architectural contract, not a real-feed claim. `VALIDATED` means structurally validated synthetic input.

UTC-aware event time and ingestion time are distinct. Receipt cannot precede occurrence. Sequences/revisions are positive bounded integers, payloads are at most 4096 encoded bytes, numbers must be finite and event attributes use a per-type allowlist. Goals/cards/shots/substitutions require a side. Known schema types cover kickoff, clocks, scores, cards, shots, possession, substitutions, explicit pauses/resumption, stoppage, full time, connection state, corrections and fictional market observations. The adapter capability matrix is returned by the status API; no genuine provider is configured.

The delivery identity is `(source_id,event_id,revision)`; receipt time does not change its fact hash. Identical redelivery is suppressed. Reusing an identity for changed facts, skipping a revision, colliding sequences or crossing a match/mode boundary is rejected. Sequence gaps and backward provider clocks are visible quality flags. Missing observations are never fabricated. Primary-source observations determine the sporting state; competing score reports remain separate with their minute and a disagreement flag.

An explicit correction references a known event, its exact revision and the same source. It cancels that fact or supplies validated replacement attributes. Reconciliation selects latest event versions, applies correction metadata and rebuilds the accepted prefix in event-time order. Previous forecast rows are not edited: append-only invalidation records identify superseded dependencies. A new unversioned goal after an authoritative final whistle cannot change the final score. A late pre-final observation can still be reconciled, and explicit versioned final-score correction remains possible.

The derived state stores the clock/period/status, observed score/cards/statistics, source identities, quality flags, delay, sequence watermarks and event/receipt cutoffs. Fixed prediction horizons stop at the known period boundary; a clock reaching ninety is not itself full time. Suspended/unknown-end states withhold unavailable mathematical outputs instead of inventing certainty.

SQLite triggers reject updates/deletes of event, state, decision, forecast, analysis, intent, invalidation and experiment rows. Mutable rows are derived current pointers, replay controls, budgets and counters. Store ownership is probed read-only before schema creation; no foreign retained database is opened for operational use. No real sporting records are read by acceptance fixtures.
