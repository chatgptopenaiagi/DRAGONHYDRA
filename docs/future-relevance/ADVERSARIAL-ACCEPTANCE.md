The module under test executes bounded same-time acquisition trajectories. Every policy receives a fresh public dictionary; the simulator instance and its hidden family, seed, rates and future outcomes remain outside that boundary. Acquisition changes visibility, not the underlying goal stream. A DEFER selection does not advance virtual time inside the pure executor. Actual acquisition quotes, the real local probability kernel and later synthetic outcome labels determine measured benefit. No fake external-service success or guessed positive result is used.
